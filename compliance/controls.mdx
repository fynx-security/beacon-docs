title: Control formulas
description: Exact scoring logic for each NIS2 Article 21 control in Beacon.
---

Status thresholds for all controls:

| Status | Score |
|--------|-------|
| Green | ≥ 80 |
| Amber | ≥ 60 |
| Red | &lt; 60 |

## 21_2_f_cis — Security configuration (CIS)

**Input:** Latest CIS snapshot `score_pct` per agent.

**Formula:** Average of all agent CIS scores. If no agents → 0% red.

**Improve:** Fix failed CIS checks; rerun `cis` module.

---

## 21_2_f_vuln — Vulnerability management

**Input:** Open critical CVEs and age since `first_detected_at`.

| Condition | Score |
|-----------|-------|
| No open critical CVEs older than 30 days | 95 |
| Critical CVEs open > 30 days | 70 |
| Critical CVEs open > 90 days | 40 |

**Improve:** Patch, upgrade packages, or mark resolved after verification.

---

## 21_2_j_inventory — Asset inventory

**Input:** Agents vs agents with `InventorySnapshot` in last **7 days**.

**Formula:** `100 × (covered_agents / total_agents)`

**Improve:** Enable inventory module (default 300s interval); ensure agents online.

---

## 21_2_b_logging — Security monitoring & logging

**Input:** Open alerts by severity and age.

| Condition | Score |
|-----------|-------|
| Any open **critical** alert | 35 |
| Open **high** alert older than 7 days | 65 |
| Open high alerts (all &lt; 7 days) | 85 |
| No open critical/high | 95 |

**Improve:** Triage alerts in Log Monitoring; resolve false positives; fix true positives.

---

## 21_2_h_credentials — Credential exposure

**Input:** Monitored emails and breach status.

| Condition | Score |
|-----------|-------|
| No emails configured | 70 |
| Emails configured | `100 × (clean_emails / total_emails)` |

**Improve:** Add corporate emails to Breach Monitor; rotate credentials for hits.

---

## Overall score

```
overall = mean(control.score_pct for control in controls)
```

Overall status uses the same green/amber/red thresholds.
