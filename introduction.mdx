## What is Beacon?

Beacon is a **compliance and security posture platform** built for organizations preparing for **NIS2**. It is not a SIEM or XDR product. Beacon answers a different question:

> *Can we show continuous evidence that our security measures are in place — and export that proof for auditors?*

Beacon connects three components:

| Component | Repository | Role |
|-----------|------------|------|
| **Beacon Dashboard** | `beacon-dashboard` | Web UI for fleet, posture, alerts, and compliance |
| **Beacon API** | `beacon-api` | Ingestion, scoring, rules engine, evidence export |
| **Beacon Host** | `beacon-host-agent` | Lightweight agent on servers and workstations |

## What Beacon collects

Beacon Host gathers **evidence**, not raw telemetry lakes:

- **CIS benchmark** scores (Linux, macOS, Windows)
- **Host vulnerabilities** (Trivy)
- **Asset inventory** (hardware, users, ports, software)
- **Security events** (auth, privilege, persistence, tamper)
- **File integrity** changes on sensitive paths (optional)

The platform maps this data to **NIS2 Article 21 controls**, surfaces open findings, and produces **exportable audit packs**.

## What Beacon is not

<Warning>
  Beacon does not certify your organization as NIS2 compliant. It automates evidence collection and posture visibility to support your compliance program.
</Warning>

- Not a replacement for legal counsel or formal certification
- Not a cloud SIEM or EDR platform
- Not repository/dependency SCA (host-level scanning only today)

## Product family

Beacon is the **compliance platform**. Future collectors will use their own names while feeding the same platform:

| Collector | Status | Purpose |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| **Beacon Host** | Available | Endpoint CIS, vulns, logs, inventory, FIM |
| **Beacon Cluster** | Planned | Kubernetes posture and evidence |
| **Other Fynx tools** | Separate products | Non-compliance tooling lives outside Beacon |
